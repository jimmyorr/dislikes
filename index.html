Import watch history

Adds your own play counts to the library and unlocks Insights. The file is read on this device and never uploaded.

Drop your Takeout .zip or watch-history.json here
How to get it from Google Takeout
  1. Open takeout.google.com and sign in with your YouTube account.
  2. Click Deselect all, then tick YouTube and YouTube Music.
  3. Click All YouTube data included, then Deselect all, and tick only history.
  4. Click Multiple formats and set History to JSON (the default, HTML, can't be read).
  5. Click Next step, keep Export once and .zip, then Create export.
  6. When Google emails you (minutes to hours), download the .zip and drop it here. No need to unzip.

History only goes back as far as YouTube kept it (check your auto-delete setting). Import again any time to refresh; the new file replaces the old one.
